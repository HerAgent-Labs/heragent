# Product Direction

## Vision

HerAgent is an open-source AI-powered business assistant that helps entrepreneurs operate and grow their businesses through specialized, permissioned agents.

## Initial agent areas

1. Finance — transaction capture, categorization, summaries, and reports.
2. Marketing — campaign concepts, content drafts, and calendars.
3. Customer support — enquiry summaries and response drafts.
4. Business planning — plans, budgets, and growth strategies.
5. Operations — tasks, reminders, and workflows.

The first implementation should prioritize finance, marketing, and customer support.

## Product guardrails

- The product must remain useful without a connected blockchain wallet.
- AI-generated suggestions are not authoritative financial records.
- Sensitive actions require human approval before execution.
- Financial calculations must be deterministic and based on authorized records.
- Global readiness means localization-ready architecture, not claiming every country's integrations at launch.
