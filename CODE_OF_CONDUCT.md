# Code of Conduct

HerAgent is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Being respectful and constructive.
- Welcoming differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report concerns privately to the contact listed in the repository's security or organization profile. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.
